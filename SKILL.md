# Workspace Switch Checklist

Writes the checklist before a team moves. Macro is the worked example: email, chat, docs, tasks and a simple CRM in one app. The code is open. The version a team would use is hosted.

## How to run

- Work through this without extra back-and-forth. Make the safest reasonable assumption, say it in one line, keep going. Only stop for something only the user has.
- Never invent a fact, figure, customer, price or quote. If it is unknown, write `[NEEDS ANSWER]` and list it at the end.
- Nothing here sends, posts, pays or changes a live account.
- If a step fails, retry it yourself and note what you changed. Do not stop at the first error.

Sign up to nothing. Connect to nothing.

## Ask

One at a time:

1. How many people would need a seat?
2. Which tools would it replace, and what does each cost today? Use their own figures.
3. Is your mail on Google Workspace, Microsoft 365, or something else?
4. Do you need single sign-on?
5. Does your data need to stay in Australia, by contract or by choice?
6. Which one team would trial it first?

## Read

Read these now, because plans and features change: https://macro.com/pricing, https://docs.macro.com/faq, and the open issues at https://github.com/macro-inc/macro/issues. Take today's numbers from those pages and give the date you read them. If a page shows a price with no currency, say that. Do not assume one.

## Write `workspace-switch-checklist.md`

Each line gets pass, fail, unknown, and the source.

1. **Seats against today's bill.** Their FAQ says every team member needs a paid seat. Seats times today's price, set beside what the replaced tools cost now. Show the working.
2. **Mail.** Only Google and Microsoft mail are supported for now. Keep that mail underneath it.
3. **Where the data sits.** Their FAQ names EU hosting and does not mention Australia. If it matters, this is a question to put to them in writing.
4. **Single sign-on.** Open request: https://github.com/macro-inc/macro/issues/5950
5. **Desktop app email link.** Open bug: https://github.com/macro-inc/macro/issues/9291
6. **Running it yourself.** Their FAQ says self-hosting has not been their focus. Treat the hosted version as the real product.
7. **What moves.** Files, history and links from each tool being replaced, and who moves them.
8. **Getting out.** How the team would export everything if the trial fails.
9. **The trial.** One team, a set length, what would count as a pass, and who decides.

Finish with a plain recommendation: trial, wait, or skip, and the one reason why. Say what would change it.
